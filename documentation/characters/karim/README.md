# Karim al-Nahhas

Person 13. Conditional entrant for machine tools, steel, and engines.

## Profile

- **Token:** `karim`
- **Country:** Egypt (`EGY`)
- **Culture:** Misri
- **Religion:** Sunni
- **Home:** Lower Egypt, with factory work in Cairo
- **Pop type:** Machinists
- **Starting age:** 24

Karim is a machinist: not the factory owner and not the unskilled labor line,
but the worker close enough to the gears to hear what will fail. He appears
when machine tools, steel mills, motor industry, or mid-century manufacturing
make skilled factory labor legible.

## Selection Intent

Karim should appear after factories stop being abstract buildings and become
machine rooms with trained hands inside them. Samier owns labor anger, Tarek
owns capital and ownership, and Karim owns skill, precision, bodily risk, and
the sound of machinery.
