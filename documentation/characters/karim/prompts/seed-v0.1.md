# Karim al-Nahhas visual seed prompts v0.1

Purpose: canonical GPT-image-2 still/portrait direction for Karim. His live events currently use validated vanilla motion plates; these prompts define custom stills for skilled machine labor.

## Identity

- Token: `karim`
- Age range: mid twenties to thirties
- Culture/religion: Misri Sunni
- Work: machinist, tool bench worker, engine mechanic
- Visual anchors: oil-blackened fingernails, calipers, gear teeth, leather apron, bandaged finger
- Palette: iron grey, oil black, brass, sweat-dark linen, forge orange
- Avoid: factory owner, anonymous laborer, futuristic machinery, heroic inventor

## Canonical Portrait

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: Common People canonical character portrait, 3:2 landscape, final crop must read at 600x400
Primary request: Generate one painted historical realism portrait-scene for Karim al-Nahhas in the Common People Victoria 3 mod.
Subject: Karim al-Nahhas, Misri Egyptian Sunni machinist around twenty-four, compact build, serious dark eyes, olive-brown skin, short black hair, rolled sleeves, worn leather apron, oil-blackened fingernails.
Scene/backdrop: Cairo machine shop, gear teeth, belt drive, tool bench, iron filings catching light.
Foreground action: Karim holds a small caliper against a metal part, his face close enough to listen to the machine.
Required object/gesture: caliper, oil-blackened fingers, and gear teeth must be legible at 600x400.
Composition/framing: close-medium, Karim at workbench, machinery close but not swallowing him.
Lighting/mood: hot workshop light, concentration, danger made ordinary.
Style/medium: painterly historical realism, textured oil-paint surface, natural anatomy, grounded materials.
Constraints: no text, no UI, no border, no watermark, no modern tools, no safety goggles.
Avoid: genius inventor pose, giant fantasy gears, clean showroom machine, owner inspecting workers.
```
