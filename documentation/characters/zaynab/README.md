# Zaynab

## Profile

Zaynab is a Misri Sunni midwife in Lower Egypt, forty-three in 1836, known by women who would not call her a doctor and men who summon her anyway when the room goes quiet. She is practical, religious without performance, and very difficult to impress. Public health reaches her only when it can stand in a courtyard and be trusted by a mother.
