Lighting/mood: cool dawn light through the doorway, warm lamplight on the key and her hands, restrained and intimate, a witness's memory rather than a heroic portrait.
Style/medium: painterly historical realism, textured oil-paint surface, natural anatomy, grounded materials, warm earth, faded indigo, dark wood, brass, paper cream.
Constraints: no text, no UI, no border, no watermark, no modern clothing, no fantasy costume, no palace, no heroic pose, no glossy digital smoothness.
Avoid: generic desert scene, Orientalist harem imagery, decorative atmosphere without the key and chest, legible writing, modern paper or furniture.
