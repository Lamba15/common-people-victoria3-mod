# Nour

## Profile

Nour is a Misri Sunni woman in Lower Egypt, thirty-one in 1836, living above a small market-room that sells cloth, lamp oil, household odds, and credit as often as goods. She is not an ideologue. Her politics are the chest key, the account paper, the room behind the shop, and the question of whose name can hold them.

## Tone
