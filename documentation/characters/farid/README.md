# Farid al-Haddad

Farid is Person 9: a Misri Sunni railway porter and errand runner in Lower Egypt.
He is conditional, not a startup face. His life becomes eligible once Egypt has
railways, electric telegraphy, or a railway building.

## Mechanical Role

- Token: `farid`
- Country: `EGY`
- Culture/religion: `misri` / `sunni`
- Home: `STATE_LOWER_EGYPT`
- Pop cohort: `laborers`
- Entry hooks: yearly, technology acquired, building built
- First appearance: person-owned chance after railway/telegraph eligibility

## Narrative Role

Farid makes infrastructure visible below the level of ministers and maps. The
railway is a shoulder bruise, a platform wind, a night lamp, a wire that makes
men lower their voices. He should appear when the country modernizes and then
share the same ambient budget as every other person.
