Purpose: canonical GPT-image-2 still/portrait direction for Farid. His live events currently use validated vanilla motion plates; these prompts define the first custom stills to generate when Farid needs intimate art.

- Age range: late teens to early twenties
- Culture/religion: Misri Sunni
- Work: railway porter, platform errand runner, telegraph messenger
- Visual anchors: cloth shoulder pad, brass hand lamp, folded telegram slip, dust on railway ballast
- Palette: coal black, Nile dust, faded blue shirt, brass lamp yellow, telegraph wire grey
