# Rashid al-Katib

Person 11. Conditional entrant for bureaucracy, records, and identity papers.

## Profile

- **Token:** `rashid`
- **Country:** Egypt (`EGY`)
- **Culture:** Misri
- **Religion:** Sunni
- **Home:** Lower Egypt, with his office life in Cairo
- **Pop type:** Bureaucrats
- **Starting age:** 31

Rashid is a low government clerk: not the minister, not the judge, just the
hand that copies names into the book. He appears when state paperwork becomes
mechanically legible in the campaign: government administration buildings,
central archives, identification documents, or the bureaucracy technology.

## Selection Intent

Rashid should not be a first-contact candidate at game start. He enters once
the campaign has made offices, archives, or identity papers a real part of
ordinary Egyptian life. His router branch is strongest when government
administration or modern records exist, and quieter otherwise.
