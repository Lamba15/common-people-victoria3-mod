# Nabil al-Haras visual seed prompts v0.1

Purpose: canonical GPT-image-2 still/portrait direction for Nabil. His live events currently use validated vanilla motion plates; these prompts define custom stills for watchman, police, and street-order scenes.

## Identity

- Token: `nabil`
- Age range: forties to sixties
- Culture/religion: Misri Sunni
- Work: night watchman, poorly paid public-order intermediary
- Visual anchors: corner lamp, staff, worn sash, station paper, bakery shutter, square dust after crowd
- Palette: lamp amber, night blue, dust grey, worn brown, police dark cloth
- Avoid: police commander, revolutionary hero, faceless soldier, riot spectacle as the only subject

## Canonical Portrait

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: Common People canonical character portrait, 3:2 landscape, final crop must read at 600x400
Primary request: Generate one painted historical realism portrait-scene for Nabil al-Haras in the Common People Victoria 3 mod.
Subject: Nabil al-Haras, Misri Egyptian Sunni night watchman in his fifties, weathered face, grey in his beard, tired dark eyes, plain dark robe, worn sash, wooden staff, small oil lamp.
Scene/backdrop: Cairo or Lower Egyptian street corner at night, bakery shutter, low wall, lane disappearing into darkness.
Foreground action: Nabil trims the corner lamp with one hand while holding his staff under his arm.
Required object/gesture: lamp flame, staff, and bakery shutter must be legible at 600x400.
Composition/framing: close-medium, Nabil foreground beside lamp, empty lane behind.
Lighting/mood: oil-lamp night, vigilance, fear of both quiet and noise.
Style/medium: painterly historical realism, textured oil-paint surface, natural anatomy, grounded materials.
Constraints: no text, no UI, no border, no watermark, no modern police uniform, no military glamour.
Avoid: heroic policeman, crowd riot panorama, detective scene, palace guard.
```
