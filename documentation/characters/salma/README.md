# Salma Farag

Person 12. Conditional entrant for electric service, telephones, and radio.

## Profile

- **Token:** `salma`
- **Country:** Egypt (`EGY`)
- **Culture:** Misri
- **Religion:** Sunni
- **Home:** Lower Egypt, with exchange work in Cairo
- **Pop type:** Clerks
- **Starting age:** 19

Salma is a switchboard clerk: not an inventor, not a minister of posts, just the
young woman whose fingers make the country's new wires briefly human. She
appears when electric service, telephone networks, radio, or electric industry
buildings make late-century communication visible in ordinary work.

## Selection Intent

Salma should not be part of the 1836 first-contact roll. She enters once the
campaign has made electricity, switchboards, or broadcast voices plausible.
Her router branch is strongest after the electric/telephone layer appears or in
the post-1880 era, and quieter otherwise.
