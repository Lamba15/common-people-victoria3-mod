# Dawud Hanna

Dawud is Person 10: a Misri Orthodox dockworker in Lower Egypt. He is a
conditional entrant tied to ports, steamships, and cargo handling rather than a
startup character.

## Mechanical Role

- Token: `dawud`
- Country: `EGY`
- Culture/religion: `misri` / `orthodox`
- Home: `STATE_LOWER_EGYPT`
- Pop cohort: `laborers`
- Entry hooks: yearly, technology acquired, building built
- First appearance: person-owned chance after port or steam eligibility

## Narrative Role

Dawud makes trade legible without turning it into an abstract market number:
rope burn, salt on the floor, manifests, cranes, steam smoke, and the fear that
machines may spare a shoulder while erasing a wage.
