# Samier visual seed prompts v0.1

Purpose: canonical GPT-image-2 still/portrait direction for Samier. His intro currently uses a validated vanilla motion plate, while the labor-law reaction has a shipped DDS; these prompts preserve his custom still direction for future accident, strike, and radicalization scenes.

## Identity

- Token: `samier`
- Age range: late teens to thirties
- Culture/religion: Misri Sunni
- Work: factory laborer, politically awakening worker
- Visual anchors: factory gate, lunch cloth, soot on collar, torn sleeve, workers' notice
- Palette: smoke grey, brick red, faded blue, oil black, paper cream
- Avoid: confident union leader too early, factory owner framing, modern worker gear

```text
Foreground action: Samier stands at the gate holding a cloth-wrapped lunch, looking at the factory doors rather than the viewer.
Required object/gesture: cloth-wrapped lunch and soot-stained collar must be legible at 600x400.
Composition/framing: close-medium, Samier foreground, factory gate behind.

Constraints: no text, no UI, no border, no watermark, no modern factory equipment.
Avoid: strike rally, heroic labor poster, owner inspection scene.
```
