# Character Sketches

Lightweight characters. Each has a personality and reactions but no multi-chapter arc. Any sketch can be promoted to an anchor (its own file, deep arc) when the time comes.

The goal is breadth: a player's run should surface a handful of these, each feeling distinct, and the pool should grow with every release.

---

## Farmers and Serfs

- **Dmitri** (RUS) -- Russian serf freed in 1861. Bewildered by freedom. Drinks. Deep-arc candidate: emancipation and its aftermath, redemption payment trap. See `../research/russia.md`.
- **Priya** (BIC/HND) -- Indian peasant under British colonial land policy. Deep-arc candidate: navigating foreign land laws and famines. See `../research/asia.md`.
- **Rosa** (Generic) -- Farmer's wife in any agrarian nation. Cares about harvests, religion, family.
- **Old Mahmoud** (EGY) -- Elderly farmer who remembers the old ways. Elder archetype. Skeptical of all change.

## Factory Workers

- **Thomas** (GBR) -- Manchester mill worker. Deep-arc candidate: Chartist movement, labor organizing. Peak of British industrialization.
- **Anya** (RUS) -- Russian woman in a state-owned factory. Exhausted, endures quietly.
- **Kofi** (Generic) -- Worker in a colonial-era mine or plantation processing. Focused on sending money home.
- **Luca** (Generic) -- Italian immigrant factory worker. Homesick, writes letters.

## Soldiers (variants of the generic soldier)

- **The Volunteer** (Generic) -- Enlisted willingly. Patriotic at first, disillusioned. Deep-arc candidate.
- **Sergeant Osman** (OTO) -- Ottoman NCO. Professional soldier, seen too much.
- **The Deserter** (Generic) -- Ran from the front. Lives in hiding, guilty and free.
- **The War Widow** (Generic) -- Husband didn't come back. Navigating life as a dependent.

## Women

- **Nour** (EGY) -- Egyptian woman. Deep-arc candidate: women's rights from no rights to property ownership. Traditional family pressures vs. changing laws. See `../research/egypt.md` (Sha'arawi, Amin, al-Fawwaz).
- **Clara** (Generic) -- Suffragist. Deep-arc candidate: political organizing, petitions, the vote.
- **Mei** (QNG) -- Chinese woman during the upheaval. Bound feet, traditional expectations, a world in revolution around her.

## Children

- **Little Ali** (Generic) -- Child laborer in a factory. Deep-arc candidate: from labor to school (or death). Sensitive, carefully handled.
- **Sophie** (Generic) -- Street orphan. Survives by wits. Observes everything, understands nothing.
- **The Schoolchild** (Generic) -- First generation to attend public school. Wonder at learning to read.

## Immigrants

- **Giuseppe** (ITA -> Generic) -- Italian emigrant. Deep-arc candidate: leaves Italy for opportunity, faces xenophobia, builds a life.
- **Fiona** (IRE -> Generic) -- Irish famine refugee. Haunted by hunger. See `../research/europe.md`.
- **Chen Wei** (QNG -> Generic) -- Chinese laborer abroad. Isolated by language, exploited, resilient.
- **The Returned** (Generic) -- Emigrant who came back. Neither home nor abroad feels right.

## Slaves and Freed People

- **Solomon** (USA) -- American slave. Deep-arc candidate: bondage, escape or emancipation, freedom's reality.
- **Maria** (BRZ) -- Brazilian slave. Deep-arc candidate: abolition movement, the day of freedom, what comes after. See `../research/americas.md`.
- **The Freedman** (Generic) -- Recently freed. Navigating a world that freed him legally but not socially.

## Clergy and Faith

- **Father Alexei** (RUS) -- Orthodox priest watching secularization. Deep-arc candidate: faith vs. modernity.
- **Imam Yusuf** (Generic) -- Village imam. Concerned about moral decay, modernization.
- **Sister Catherine** (Generic) -- Nun running a charity hospital. Sees suffering, fights bureaucracy.

## Servants and Domestics

- **James** (GBR) -- Butler in an aristocratic household. Sees power from below. Discreet, observant.
- **Aisha** (Generic) -- Domestic maid. Overhears everything, says nothing.
- **The Cook** (Generic) -- Household cook. Measures prosperity by what's in the pantry.

## Shopkeepers and Artisans

- **Hans** (Generic) -- Master craftsman. Watching factories destroy his trade. Bitter, proud.
- **Madame Dupont** (FRA) -- Parisian shopkeeper. Political opinions about everything. Loves gossip.
- **The Baker** (Generic) -- Measures the nation's health by grain prices. Panics at inflation.

## Teachers and Intellectuals

- **Professor Okonkwo** (Generic) -- Teacher in newly established school. Deep-arc candidate: first generation of literacy, what education means for a nation.
- **The Student** (Generic) -- University student, idealistic. Quotes philosophers, knows nothing of real life yet.
- **The Journalist** (Generic) -- Newspaper writer. Comments on everything. May become an Agitator.

## Sailors and Dockers

- **Captain's Boy** (Generic) -- Young sailor. Sees the world from port to port.
- **The Docker** (Generic) -- Port laborer. First to know about foreign affairs (ships bring news).
- **The Fisherman** (Generic) -- Coastal villager. Doesn't care about politics. Cares about the sea.

## Miners

- **Big Jack** (Generic) -- Coal miner. Deep-arc candidate: dangerous work, lung disease, mine collapse, strikes. The underground world.
- **The Prospector** (Generic) -- Gold rush character. Chases fortune, finds mud.
- **The Miner's Wife** (Generic) -- Waits for the whistle that says the shift ended safely.

## Criminals and Outcasts

- **The Thief** (Generic) -- Steals to survive. Sees law reform from the wrong side. Prison, punishment, maybe redemption.
- **The Debtor** (Generic) -- Imprisoned for debt. A system that punishes poverty.

## Elders and Storytellers

- **Grandmother** (Generic) -- Old woman who remembers the world before. Living memory. "In my day..."
- **The Bard** (Generic) -- Storyteller, folk singer, or local historian. Narrates the mod itself -- meta-character who tells the stories of others.
