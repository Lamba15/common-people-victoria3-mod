# Russia -- Common People Research

## Serf Life Before 1861

- Mandatory labor (barshchina) for landowners, typically 3 days/week (often violated -- more)
- Fed stale bread and buckwheat gruel; minimal housing provided by landowners
- Marriage strictly controlled by Orthodox Church; minimum age 13 for women, 15 for men
- Landowners could buy, sell, mortgage serfs as commodities
- Punishments: whippings, isolation, forced labor; complete landowner control

## The Emancipation (1861)

- 23+ million serfs freed by Emancipation Manifesto
- Gained rights to marry without consent, own property, own businesses

### The Redemption Payment Trap
- Government advanced 75% of land cost to landlords as bonds
- Peasants repaid government WITH INTEREST over 49 years (not cancelled until 1907!)
- Payments so high peasants sold ALL grain just to pay -- nothing left to eat
- Most received LESS land than they tilled before emancipation
- Debt passed to children -- generational trap

### The Commune Trap
- Communes replaced landlords as controllers
- Peasants couldn't sell land or leave without commune permission
- Communes rarely granted permission (burden falls on remaining members)
- Former household serfs got NO land -- worst off of all

## Factory Life (Moscow and St. Petersburg, 1890s-1900s)

- St. Petersburg: 1,033,600 (1890) -> 1,905,600 (1910)
- Average 16 people per apartment, 6 per room; no running water
- Factory work: 13-14 hours/day, sometimes 17-18; dangerous monotonous work
- Food: stale bread and buckwheat gruel in crowded meal-houses
- Real wages declined 20% in 1904

## Bloody Sunday (January 22, 1905)

- Father Georgy Gapon led 3,000+ workers to Winter Palace with petition to Tsar
- Carried religious icons, sang "God Save the Tsar"
- Eyewitness: "unusual bright January sunshine, trusting, expectant faces, the fateful signal, pools of blood on the white snow, the whips, the dead, the injured, children shot"
- Soldiers fired into packed crowds; at Narva Gate 40 killed
- Father Gapon: "There is no God anymore, there is no Tsar"
- Cossacks slashed marchers with sabres; cannons on Nevsky Prospect
- ~200 dead, ~800 wounded
- Destroyed the illusion that the Tsar would help workers

## Event Ideas

- Serf discovers emancipation means 49 years of debt
- Commune refuses to let a freed serf leave for the city
- Household serf freed with nothing -- sleeps in the street
- Factory worker in St. Petersburg: 16 people in one apartment
- Bloody Sunday: a worker carries his icon to the palace, trusting the Tsar. Comes home bleeding.
