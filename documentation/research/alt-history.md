# Alt-History Scenarios for Common People

Victoria 3 is an alt-history game. Every path below is something a player might do. For each scenario, we describe how COMMON PEOPLE's lives would differ.

## Early Communism

### Paris Commune Survives (1871)
- For two months, workers controlled Paris: self-policing, rent remission, child labor abolished, workers took over abandoned enterprises
- If it survived and spread: workers' councils across Europe, no need for Bolshevik model
- Common people: no rent payments, children in school, community self-governance

### Chartists Win in 1840s Britain
- Universal male suffrage, equal districts, secret ballot, paid MPs
- Common people: working-class voices in Parliament 50 years early, factory legislation decades earlier

### Egyptian Workers' Revolution (1880s instead of Russia 1917)
- Egypt had 30,000+ industrial workers, corvee resentment, foreign exploitation
- An Arab socialist state 37 years before Lenin, controlling Suez Canal
- Common people: workers' councils, factory protections, anti-colonial movement driven by labor

### Robert Owen's Cooperatives Become Dominant
- New Lanark: 10-hour days, higher wages, no child labor, free education
- Eight hours labor, eight hours recreation, eight hours rest (Owen's 1817 slogan)
- Common people: own their enterprises, profits shared, democratic decisions on hours/conditions

## Fast Monarchical Modernization

### Ottoman Empire Industrializes Like Japan
- Japan succeeded through protectionism and social reform
- Ottoman failure: 1838 free trade treaty flooded markets with cheap European goods
- If Ottomans had protected domestic industry: peasants experience factory work but also preservation of Islamic-traditional values

### Russia Abolishes Serfdom in 1836 (instead of 1861)
- 25 years earlier: peasants can migrate to factories sooner, faster industrialization
- But likely harsher redemption terms, faster rural poverty
- Common people: freedom earlier, but same debt trap, just faster

### China's Self-Strengthening Movement Succeeds
- Actually built: 65,000 rifles, 742 cannons, 15 ships, railways, telegraphs
- Failed due to unequal treaties, corruption, no unified policy
- If succeeded: Chinese-owned mills competing with Western goods, less colonial extraction

## Anti-Colonial Industrialization

### Egypt Stays Independent and Industrializes
- Historian Lockman: Egypt could have matched Japan or USA
- Egyptian middle class, local industrial ownership, profits staying local
- Common people: higher wages (profits not exported), state education investment, economic dignity

### India Industrializes Without British Rule
- Pre-colonial India: 25% of world GDP, sophisticated economy
- British de-industrialized India (25% of textiles -> 2%)
- If independent: Indian-owned textile mills, railways for domestic trade, wages stay in India

### Africa Not Carved Up
- Pre-colonial African kingdoms had education, trade, political systems
- If development continued: modernization on African terms, less extraction of people and resources

## Early Women's Rights (50 Years Earlier)

### Women's Suffrage in the 1860s
- 1840s: 75% of Lowell textile mills were women working 73-hour weeks
- If suffrage in 1860s: women MPs by 1865, factory legislation 20+ years earlier
- Common people: women have legal voice in wage disputes, children's factory work banned sooner

### Universal Female Education from 1840s
- Girls attend school alongside boys
- Higher literacy, women in skilled trades, daughters understand contracts
- Family decision-making more equal

## Slavery Timing

### Global Abolition by 1840
- No American Civil War trauma, no Reconstruction failure
- Free labor workers in American South, Caribbean, Brazil
- Common people: ex-enslaved become wage workers with legal protections immediately

### Slavery Continues Until 1900
- 40 more years of plantation system
- Northern/European workers face even more competition from slave labor
- Common people everywhere: wages depressed by slave labor competition

## Technology Acceleration

### Germ Theory Discovered in 1830s (instead of 1860s)
- Semmelweis's handwashing reduced childbed fever from 10-20% to near zero
- If implemented in 1830s: massive mortality reduction, women survive childbirth at 10x rate
- Factory accident mortality drops, industrial workers' life expectancy jumps 10-15 years
- Children born to industrial workers have 70% better survival

### Railways Reach Egypt in 1830s
- Common people: agricultural products reach factories faster, worker migration easier, wages for transport workers

## Religious/Social Alt-History

### Islamic Modernism Wins (Muhammad Abduh's Vision)
- Islam as "moral basis of a modern and progressive society"
- Female education, modern law respecting Islamic values, scientific education in mosques
- Common people: girls attend religious schools learning modern subjects, no forced choice between "traditional Islam" and "Western secularism"

### Catholic Church Supports Workers' Rights in 1840s (instead of 1891)
- Parish-based unions, church-sanctioned strikes
- Common people: religious authority supports labor organization, higher wages seen as Christian duty
