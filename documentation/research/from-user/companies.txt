This page discusses the mechanics of companies; for the power bloc principle, see Power bloc § Companies.
Companies represent particularly notable corporations or joint ventures. Each company is associated with one or several types of building in a country. The company can own and build levels of these buildings and provide bonuses to throughput and construction efficiency. Companies potentially provide additional national bonuses if the company is sufficiently productive.

Companies
 Companies III.

Each company has certain requirements before it can be established, typically building a minimum level of one of its associated buildings. Flavored companies tend to have stricter requirements, such as constructing the requisite building in a specific state. Additionally, 5 levels of an associated building in one state must be available to transfer to the company's ownership. This can be from private owners, nationally-owned, or a mix of both.

When a company is established, it cannot be disbanded for five years. Similarly, after a company is disbanded, it cannot be reestablished for four years. Companies which own a country cannot be disbanded.

Company headquarter
Each company has an associated Building company headquarter.png Company Headquarter building. This is an ownership building similar to Building financial district.png Financial Districts and Building manor houses.png Manor Houses, but associated specifically with a particular company. When a company is established, its headquarter is immediately built in the state with enough eligible buildings and the highest productivity rating compared to other eligible states. The headquarter has as many levels as the company owns building levels; these are automatically added and removed. Company Headquarters have only a single production method – depending on the current law, power bloc principles and the company's ownership type, which have the effects listed below.

Regional headquarters
With the investment rights charter a company can establish a secondary regional HQ in a foreign country, which the country of origin has a Treaty investment rights.png Investment Rights agreement with. The regional HQ will be established as soon as they own 5 buildings in the target country. The regional HQ will then be able to buy up, construct or expand buildings in that country using that country's own investment pool. The regional HQ will then send some of their earnings back to the main HQ's investment pool. On the other hand, the country where the regional HQ has been established, will get access to potential prestige goods as well as the company construction and throughput bonuses.

Prosperity
Companies start with 0 prosperity. The current prosperity changes towards the target prosperity weekly by 0.2 to 1 depending on the number of fully employed building levels the company owns. With 20 or more fully employed levels, prosperity increases weekly by 1, and with 40 or more levels it decreases weekly by 0.2. These values scale down or up with fewer employed levels; for example, with only 10 fully employed levels, a company's prosperity would increase weekly by 0.6 or decrease by 0.8.

Target prosperity is set by a combination of company productivity, number of employed levels, and Political popularity.png popularity of the Role executive.png executive. Productivity gives 50 times the companies relative productivity. This is equal to the average productivity of the companies owned building levels compared to the average productivity of all associated building levels not owned by the company. Each fully employed building level gives another 1, up to 50. Finally, each point of the executive's popularity gives 0.2, from −20 to 20. The maximum target cannot exceed 100, but it can be "buffered" if the total sum is over 100. For example, a company with a relative productivity of 1.5 (50% more productive than global average for its building types), with 10 employed levels and an executive with −10 popularity would have a prosperity target of Failed to parse (SVG (MathML can be enabled via browser plugin): Invalid response ("Math extension cannot connect to Restbase.") from server "https://en.wikipedia.org/api/rest_v1/":): {\displaystyle (50 \cdot 1.5) + 10 + (0.2 \cdot -10) = 75 + 10 - 2 = 83}

When a company reaches 100 current prosperity, its prosperity bonus activates. If the company later drops below 75 prosperity, its bonus deactivates. If a company is disbanded and later re-established, it starts with 0 prosperity again.

Company countries
Countries created by the Company colonization rights.png Colonization Rights charter are "owned" by that company and are Chartered Company chartered company subjects of the company's owning country. They get the construction efficiency and throughput benefits of the company, which apply to nationally owned buildings in addition to those owned by the company's HQ. For buildings associated with the company, these are the full benefits, though scaled to the company country's prestige, not the overlord's prestige. For other buildings, the benefits are reduced to only 1/5 of the full amount, but this applies to all buildings, even government funded buildings. If a company country becomes independent, it loses the connection to the owning company. Company countries can only be Chartered Company chartered companies and can only have Chartered Company chartered company subjects.

Flavored companies
Main article: List of flavored companies
Many historical companies are represented as flavored companies. The AI is more likely to establish a flavored company over a basic one. These companies are typically tied to specific states or cultures. As such they are heavily associated with certain countries, but those tied to states are not technically limited to specific countries as in theory any country can conquer any state. If the state required is a split state, it is not possible that two countries can found the same flavored company.
